Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how Mizo Assistant ("the assistant", "we") handles your information. Mizo Assistant is an invite-only personal assistant that you use through a chat app. In this policy, "Google user data" means data the assistant receives from your Google account through Google APIs after you sign in with Google and approve access.

1. What we collect

  • Your chat messages with the assistant, including voice notes (turned into text on our own server) and files or pictures you send. We also keep the assistant's replies and the results of the tools it used to answer you.
  • Account identifiers from the chat app, such as your Telegram user ID and display name, so the assistant knows which user is writing.
  • Google user data, only if you connect Google:
    • Google Calendar: your events on calendars you own (title, time, location, description, attendees), your calendar's time zone, and your calendar settings needed to show times correctly.
  • An OAuth token that lets the assistant reach the Google services you approved. We never see or store your Google password.
  • Technical logs: timestamps, which tools ran, errors, and usage counts. Logs may contain short previews of messages.

2. How we use it

We use your information only to provide the features you ask for in the chat. For example, we answer "what's on my calendar tomorrow", create or move an event when you ask, and send you reminders you set up. We also use it to keep the service secure and working: to debug errors and to monitor cost and performance. We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not build profiles for anyone else.

Actions that affect other people, such as inviting guests to an event, are shown to you first and done only after you approve them in the chat.

3. How Google user data is used

Mizo Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features you see in the chat: reading, creating and updating calendar events and reading your calendar time zone at your request.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, including retargeting, personalised or interest-based ads.
  • We do not sell Google user data, and we do not transfer it to data brokers or information resellers.
  • We do not use Google user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalised or non-personalised AI or machine-learning models. Our AI providers (below) process it only to produce your answer and are contractually barred from training their models on it.
  • Humans do not read your Google user data, except: with your explicit permission for a specific item (for example, when you ask us to help with a problem); when needed for security, such as investigating abuse; to comply with the law; or when the data is aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.
  • We transfer Google user data only to the service providers listed in section 4, only as needed to provide the features you asked for, or as required by law.

4. Who processes your data

The assistant runs on our own server. To understand and answer your messages, the assistant sends the conversation, including any Google user data needed for your request, to an AI model provider. These providers act as our service providers:

ProviderWhat it receivesWhy
Anthropic (Claude API)The conversation, tool results (which can include calendar details you asked about)The main AI model that writes the assistant's answers. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API data is not used to train its models.
Google Cloud Vertex AI (Gemini)The same, only when the main model is unavailable or a task is handed to a larger modelBackup AI model. Under Google Cloud's terms, this data is not used to train Google's models.
Telegram (or WhatsApp, if you chat there)Your messages and the assistant's repliesThe chat app you use to talk to the assistant, under that app's own privacy policy.
Google (Calendar API)Requests to read or change your calendarTo perform the actions you asked for.

We do not share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

5. Where it is stored and for how long

  • Conversation history, logs and OAuth tokens are stored on our server, in access-restricted storage. Tokens are kept in files only the service can read.
  • Backups of the server stay on the same machine and are rotated weekly; a backup is overwritten within about five weeks.
  • We keep your data while you use the assistant. If you stop using it or ask us to delete it, we delete it as described below.
  • AI providers keep request data only for the limited period set in their commercial terms, for example for abuse monitoring, and then delete it.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Disconnect Google at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The assistant then loses access immediately. We delete the stored token when we see it no longer works, or sooner if you ask.
  • Delete your data: write to mizo.assistant.ai@gmail.com from the account you use, or ask the assistant in the chat to forward a deletion request. We delete your conversation history, stored Google user data and tokens within 30 days, and they leave our rotating backups within a further five weeks.
  • Copy of your data: you can ask for a copy of what we store about you at the same address.

7. Security

The assistant's services are not reachable from the public internet, and administrative access goes only through a private VPN. Connections to Google and to the AI providers are encrypted (HTTPS). Tools that change something, such as creating an event, ask for your approval. Text found in emails, calendar events or web pages is treated as data and never as instructions to the assistant. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you.

8. Children

Mizo Assistant is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the chat before the change applies to you.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: mizo.assistant.ai@gmail.com.
