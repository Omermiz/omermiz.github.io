Mizo Assistant

Mizo Assistant is a personal assistant that you talk to in a chat app (Telegram). You write to it in plain language, in Hebrew or English. It answers questions, keeps reminders, and helps with everyday errands.

The service is invite-only and runs for a small group of users. You cannot sign up on this site.

What it does with your Google account

Connecting a Google account is optional. If you choose to connect it, you sign in with Google and approve each permission on Google's own consent screen. The assistant then uses your Google data only to do what you ask in the chat:

  • Google Calendar. It shows you your upcoming events ("what do I have tomorrow?"). When you ask, it creates or moves events. It reads your calendar's time zone so that times are correct, and it changes the time zone only when you ask.
  • Before anything that touches other people, such as inviting guests to an event, the assistant shows you exactly what it will do and waits for your approval in the chat.

Your Google data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. The Privacy Policy explains what is stored, who processes it, and how to delete it.

Disconnecting

You can remove the assistant's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also ask us to delete your data by writing to omermiz@gmail.com.
